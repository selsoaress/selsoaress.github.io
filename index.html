Fault-Resilient Adaptive Offloading with Priority Buffers for Early-Exit Deep Neural Networks

IWSF+SHIFT Workshop 2025

Some buffer architectures were presented for routing samples to the cloud for classification when confidence levels at the edge are insufficient. Otherwise, processing is performed locally on the device.

Paper

Offloading Adaptativo em Redes Neurais com Saídas Antecipadas: Equilíbrio e Compartilhamento de Recursos Através de Multi-Armed Bandits

Brazilian Symposium on Computer Networks (SBRC 2026)

This study proposes a dynamic threshold adjustment for Early Exit Neural Networks (EENNs) to optimize edge-to-cloud offloading. To overcome the limitations of fixed thresholds in dynamic, multi-device environments, the work models device interactions using stochastic games and multi-armed bandits, factoring in energy and server constraints to achieve approximate Nash equilibria.
